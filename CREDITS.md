## Foundation and libraries


Melodee: Kerem Kilic / Ellic Studio (AGPL-3.0-only). No Melodee firmware is installed by this instrument.
Native MPC plug-in SDK, Open MPC wrappers and Schwung API: their original contributors (MIT).
nlohmann/json: Niels Lohmann and contributors (MIT). Desktop interface: JUCE contributors (AGPL-3.0 route and retained dependency notices).
Rubik and Inter Tight fonts: original font authors (SIL Open Font License 1.1).

Attribution summary. Full copyright and license texts, dependency notices and pinned source provenance are retained in Licenses/ and SOURCE-INDEX.json.
SOURCE-INDEX.json records pinned repositories and commits. Content/build-receipts.json identifies compiled components.
Original copyright notices are preserved, including additional source and asset authors. The summary does not replace those notices or corresponding source obligations.
No sampled commercial breaks are bundled. Prophet Sequential data and Elixir Erica/Hrtl banks are excluded. Rhythmer-authored replacement sounds: Jazzy Rhythmer.
